# DOTY PM V8.0 — release and local review

Preview: **http://localhost:8766/?demo&sync** (127.0.0.1 also works). Synthetic only. The V8 backend and approved owner migration were deployed on 2026-10-03 after explicit approval. Existing credentials, KV source records and current sharing grants were retained. Existing synthetic demo edits are preserved.

## Navigation and project history

Five top-level tabs: **Calendar · Projects · Labour · Contacts · Settings**. Calendar contains Day, Week and Month. Day retains today's overdue/tasks/shopping semantics; Week shows seven days from its anchor; Month retains the schedule grid and selected-day detail. Date stepping uses local calendar arithmetic through DST, not 24-hour timestamp jumps. Punch stays at the top. No external calendar sync.

Projects defaults to Active. **Project → Project settings → Archive project** uses an inline confirmation. **Projects → Archived → open project → Project settings → Restore project** is reversible. Archival does not delete projects, notes, items/archives, legacy time, financial history, phases or contact links. Archived projects leave active calendar/task collectors, Labour/new-entry pickers and new contact-link choices; existing contact links can still open their history. Financial history remains readable, with new changes blocked until Restore. No deadline triggers automatic project archival. The misleading tracker-only “Delete ALL data”/starter-reset controls were removed.

Contacts is the sole supplier/subtrade directory entry point. Add/Edit/Search/Archive/Restore remains owner private. People and effective day/hour wage history remain in Labour. Names have stable IDs; rate changes do not rewrite recorded labour/proposals. Archived people require explicit verified historical review or Restore. Legacy hours remain separate, unbilled and readable; no days/hours conversion. Original time routes and all 17 original MCP tools remain intact.

## CAD and expense billing

Dollar labels mean CAD. No currency setup, selectors or currency buckets appear in the new-entry workflow. New MCP rows omit currency and normalize to CAD; unsupported new currency rejects. Internal legacy currency fields remain for preservation. Non-CAD history is flagged and excluded from CAD totals; non-CAD proposals cannot be accepted as CAD. Backups preserve original foreign evidence with warnings, without conversion/relabeling.

Expenses distinguish purchase subtotal, purchase tax/gross, markup snapshot, and client pre-tax/tax/gross. **New projects** have a 15% markup default; existing projects retain at-cost defaults/history until explicitly changed. Configure defaults in **Project → Expenses → Project expense pricing**. Each entry can override the rate. Markup applies before purchase tax. Client tax treatment is explicit: Ontario HST 13%, no client tax, or a manually entered amount. A known receipt tax amount (including explicitly verified 0) is required for new charged billing; the app never divides gross by 1.13 to guess missing tax. Client-purchased/nonchargeable expenses contribute cost but no client charge.

Approved synthetic regression: purchase subtotal $1,000 + purchase HST $130; 15% markup $150; client pre-tax $1,150 + client HST $149.50 = **$1,299.50**. At 0% the total is **$1,130**. Purchase HST is not added again to the client-tax base. Markup is not accounting profit margin. The selectable Ontario rate was checked against the [CRA charge/collect guidance](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/gst-hst-businesses/charge-collect-which-rate.html); the app does not infer tax applicability from a vendor/category/location. No labour-tax policy or invoice issuance was added.

Summary keeps actual costs, client expense charges including recorded tax, markup, known client expense tax, labour charges as recorded, receipts and account balance separate from private worker accrual/payout/outstanding. Historical client-tax breakdowns remain unknown when not recorded. Account balance is recorded charges less receipts, **not issued invoice receivables**. Defaults affect future entries only; existing billing/rate/tax snapshots and original source evidence remain unchanged.

## One complete backup workflow

**Settings → Backup & restore** is the only download/import surface. Separate ledger, Contacts and legacy-hour exports/imports were removed. The versioned `doty-account-backup` v8 manifest contains complete tracker and private-store inventory plus SHA-256 part checksums. It includes owned projects/notes/schedule/tasks/shopping/item archives/project archive flags, legacy time, accepted/voided financial rows, pending/resolved proposals, provenance/checkpoints/audit/operation receipts, phases/default pricing, People/wage history/archive, Contacts/links/archive and safe non-secret restore settings. Auth/passwords/tokens/users and live sharing permissions are excluded. Private backups contain wages/contacts; they are not client-facing documents.

Export refuses partial discovery or failed validation, unresolved write intents, unsynced remote tracker differences, and malformed source storage. Local review discovers every owner ledger file, including deleted-project/orphan history. It never silently skips a failed store. Import validates schema/IDs/amounts/billing snapshots/inventory/checksums before a preview with incoming project names, matching IDs, removal warnings and explicit replacement confirmation. It replaces a whole account rather than merging money records. It rejects changes since preview and cross-owner full restores. Original notes are not parsed into transactions.

The shared **local** review serializes API operations and uses a durable multi-store recovery journal: record before-images, apply all stores, update in-memory state only on success, advance revision fences, append restore audit, retain recovery snapshots. Partial failure rolls back. Startup rolls back an interrupted apply before opening stores; lost-response replay/status and browser reload recovery avoid duplicate restore. A separate browser journal coordinates tracker completion. Browser-only mode also retains before-images. This is local recovery machinery, not a claim of a distributed Cloudflare transaction.

A **v7.2 tracker backup** is recognized as tracker-only: preview says it lacks finance, People and Contacts. Its original notes/legacy source data are retained; existing private stores/history/links stay intact. It never fabricates expenses, labour people/dates/rates, missing payments or tax. Real-data conversion is handled in a separate private migration review, with source verification and human reconciliation. An explicitly reviewed owner migration has been imported into production. Private sources and recovery copies remain outside this repository and have not been used to clear the shared synthetic preview. Unknown/repeated days, corrections, cumulative snapshots and unexplained gaps require source review; totals never become balancing transactions.

## Backend, privacy and release boundary

Frontend base `e678403` (v7.2). The captured live Worker remains byte-identical: SHA256 `d078c5e9c82751cb2531677628d2b74d0f00b8632459e71b718221b04a59767d`. `backend/worker-review.mjs` integrates that captured source and is the deployed V8 backend. The legacy/local adapter retains split stores. Fresh production V8 uses one owner Durable Object with logical tracker, project, People and Contacts partitions and exact raw-owned project checks. Worker pay never enters `/data`, shared project shadows, original project tools/time reports or `as` views. Transactional revisions/receipts protect concurrent edits and uncertain retries; authentication/users/tokens remain in the original KV; fresh V8 tracker data resides in the owner object through a compatible adapter.

There are **20 added MCP tools / 37 total**, including project expense defaults. Browser and MCP share validation/math/snapshot semantics. Human source acceptance/unvoid and backup restore are unavailable to MCP. Running totals remain evidence; repeated undated days may have distinct provenance occurrences; corrections are atomic and retain history.

Production-compatible **`OWNER_ACCOUNT_MODE="fresh-v8"`** is implemented in `backend/owner-account.mjs`. `GET /account/backup` exports a coherent whole-account snapshot from one transaction. `POST /account/backup` supports prepare/apply/status/recovery with the same reviewed replacement UI, owner/role authorization, schema/checksum validation, expected-payload hash, stable operation IDs, atomic commit, durable replay receipt and retained pre-restore complete backup. All tracker, finance, People and Contacts requests route to the same owner object. Restore advances each store revision, a per-owner generation and tracker CAS timestamp atomically. Requests that obtained permissions/rates before a restore are fenced. No HTTP/KV/other-DO calls occur inside the commit. Chunked tracker/inventory/proposal/recovery storage avoids per-value limits; inventory reads page across more than 1,000 keys. At most one prepared and one latest committed full journal remain; older receipts become small tombstones (410, token cannot be reused). The recovery action returns the previous complete bundle. Preparation and cancellation never overwrite account data. Reviewed files are limited to 20 MB; private records to 100 KB and stored keys to 1,000 bytes, rejected during preparation before replacement. Cloudflare runtime load/capacity validation has not been run locally.

The previous split layout cannot enumerate unregistered old/orphan DOs or atomically overwrite KV plus several DOs. Its legacy export still refuses uncertified inventory and its restore is unavailable. That is no longer the production V8 architecture: the owner object provides authoritative inventory, including orphan accounts, with no certification flag. The review’s FINANCE_ACCOUNTS Durable Object binding/class is reused under a new owner-name discriminator. The separately approved initial FINANCE_ACCOUNTS SQLite binding/class migration was deployed through the existing dashboard session. No credentials or paid subscription were created; the existing Workers Free plan, KV binding, origin and password binding were retained. This coordinator adds no second binding or service.

**Cutover:** explicitly choose fresh V8 owner mode at the approved release. Existing KV tracker and split DOs are untouched and are not auto-imported. Existing authentication and project-sharing logic remain; full backup excludes external sharing grants and other owners' private records. Restore the supplied V7 tracker-only file and reviewed financial handoff, or a complete V8 bundle, through the one Settings workflow. An existing split-store installation must first obtain a verified complete inventory/export while writes are paused; do not label a partial export complete. Rolling back deployment is unsafe after new V8 writes unless the owner snapshot is reconciled first: old code reads the old split/KV data. Fresh-mode account deletion is refused before any user/account mutation until its lifecycle is separately reviewed; no credentials are changed by backup operations.

Production runtime checks verified current-source preservation, authenticated complete export, prepare/apply, atomic owner activation and independent read-back of tracker and private inventory. Distributed load/capacity and actual iPhone Safari/standalone/keyboard QA remain unverified. Source conversion and deployment were explicitly approved. `backend/wrangler.review.example.toml` has no secrets/namespace IDs and was not deployed.

## Reproduce and evidence

No build/install step. `node local-preview.mjs` serves public assets only, binds loopback and rejects external Host/Origin. `.preview/` is ignored. The active server is detached from the task terminal, with `.preview/server.pid` and `server.log`; it survives task completion but must be restarted after Mac reboot/process termination. No startup service was installed.

Run the ten Node regression files listed in TEST_RESULTS.md. `node redesign-ui.test.cjs` starts its own ephemeral loopback server and temporary headless Chrome profile, runs actual UI/backup/HST/mobile checks, and stops both. It never uses the user's Chrome profile or existing preview data. Chrome is already installed; no debugger network port or external service is exposed.

Latest evidence: `UI_REDESIGN_RESULTS.json`, `preview-v8-calendar.jpg`, `preview-v8-contacts.jpg`, `preview-v8-expenses.jpg`, `preview-v8-backup.jpg`, `preview-v8-mobile.jpg`. These are synthetic desktop Chromium simulations, not physical iPhone proof. `../doty-upgrade.patch` is the complete diff; `../worker-upgrade.patch` is the small integration diff against the unchanged capture.

Final review hardening: valid checksums do not bypass phase name/key/schema, financial creation timestamp, or non-null tracker record validation. Rejected preparations leave every account store unchanged. Genuinely new projects created by browser, MCP add_project, broadcast creation and legacy HTTP auto-create routes carry the V8 creation marker; existing projects and shared overlays are not re-tagged. The original 17 tool names and authorization remain preserved.

Stored-record preflight also rejects null/malformed finance, People and Contacts audits; operation fingerprint/result contracts; proposal IDs/wrappers/resolutions; checkpoints; source provenance; wage history and directory field types before staging a restore. Supported historical audits may omit ID/actor/timestamps but must retain a non-negative integer revision. Regression cases verify failed preparations leave the complete account, a prior prepared proposal and the last recovery backup unchanged, with no applicable restore token.

Release activation protection: production configuration uses OWNER_ACTIVATE_ON_RESTORE=true. Unmigrated owners continue reading/writing their existing KV; private finance waits for their approved migration. The authorized owner’s legacy source is copied into a private staging snapshot for export/preview, then rechecked at apply. Preparation briefly fences tracker writes; cancellation releases the fence. Tracker/private stores and active owner routing switch together at commit. Other owners are not reset or automatically enrolled. Keep the existing binding/source and verify a quiet migration window/current-source match before cutover.

Deployment preflight: preserve current server-owned sharing grants on matching project/kind/item IDs during restore; backups cannot import grants. Restore refuses if current sharing changes after prepare. The uploaded source exactly matched the privately backed-up production owner record at preflight.

Legacy correction: a replacement may retain an already unassigned phase only when the target had no phase ID and the original phase text is unchanged. This prevents requiring a fabricated phase merely to correct a historic pay snapshot. New rows still require a phase; a known phase cannot be erased through this exception.

An owner-confirmed worker payout can be recorded through the authenticated financial API with an unknown date and no phase allocation. Such an account settlement does not create a bank transfer, infer a payment method or assign payment to a work phase. Replay-safe operation receipts prevent duplicate settlements; invalid provided phases still reject. Synthetic regression coverage verifies the undated, unallocated payout and unchanged client billing.
